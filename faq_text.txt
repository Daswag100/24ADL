Service questions
Q: What services do you offer?
A: Stock audit, stock verification, warehouse inspection, reconciliation, inventory control review, discrepancy analysis, and staff training.
Q: What is the difference between stock audit and stock verification?
A: Stock audit is the broader process of checking inventory, records, storage, and control. Stock verification is the physical confirmation of what is actually on ground.
Q: Do you work with manual records and digital records?
A: Yes. We can work with bin cards, stock sheets, Excel records, ERP reports, barcode systems, and other inventory records.
Process questions
Q: How does a 24ADL audit work?
A: We begin with an understanding of the site and stock type, then conduct physical verification, compare findings with records, identify discrepancies, and prepare a report with recommendations.
Q: Do you provide a report after the audit?
A: Yes. We provide a clear report of findings, observations, variances, and practical recommendations.
Q: Can you handle large warehouses?
A: Yes. We can work with small, medium, and large warehouses depending on the scope of engagement.
Q: Will our information remain confidential?
A: Yes. Confidentiality is taken seriously. Client information is handled with care and shared only with authorized persons.
